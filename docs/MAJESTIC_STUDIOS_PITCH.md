# MAJESTIC STUDIOS × LEMURIA LIFE — STUDIO PRESENTATION

## A new mythology for a new era
**One Origin. Seven Powers. Five Ages. Infinite Stories.** An interconnected cinematic universe of heroic journeys, ancient memory, ecological imagination and the courage to create a different future.

## The opportunity
Develop original IP across feature films, limited and ongoing series, animation, publishing, games and immersive storytelling. Each entry offers a satisfying character arc while contributing to the larger narrative.

## Audience promise
Emotional, accessible heroes; epic myth grounded in recognizable choices; hopeful yet morally complex futures; worlds designed for cinematic scale and intimate storytelling.

## Three-film flagship arc
1. **The First Light** — the world and its hidden inheritance awaken.
2. **The Great Fracture** — a tragic choice between control and freedom reshapes civilization.
3. **The New Dawn** — intergenerational heroes decide what a future civilization can become.

## Supporting productions
Architect of Light; Memory of the Ocean; Daughter of Fire; The Seedkeepers; The Mountain Code; The Frequency; Between Worlds; The Worldbuilders; The Convergence. Together with the three flagship stories these form a twelve-production development slate, not a completed production schedule.

## Production ecosystem
Majestic Studios is envisioned as a creative base for film, animation, sound, workshops and interactive media, potentially connected to Black Mountain Ranch and Lemuria Life. Its aesthetic should be earth-toned, refined, spiritual and harmonious with nature — not a resort or theme park.

## Potential partners
Screenwriters, showrunners, producers, filmmakers, visual-development artists, animation partners, distributors, financiers, cultural consultants and interactive studios.

## Current status and next steps
Development concept. Cast and filmmaker suggestions are aspirational. No production financing, cast attachments, distribution, land control or entitlements are represented as completed. Prioritize screenplay development, original concept art, franchise rights structure, a compelling teaser and a realistic first-feature budget.
