# LEMURIA UNIVERSE — CANON & DEVELOPMENT BIBLE

**One Origin. Seven Powers. Five Ages. Infinite Stories.**

> Original fictional development material. This is a detailed treatment bible, **not twelve completed shooting scripts**. No actors are attached, and casting examples are exploratory only.

## Franchise premise
What if humanity once knew how to live in harmony with all life, lost that knowledge, and is now being called to remember? Each story stands alone; every choice echoes across generations. The franchise combines ecological imagination, ancient memory, speculative technology and human conflict without presenting its fictional mythology as historical fact.

## Five ages
1. **First Harmony:** flourishing civilization aligns consciousness, ecology and technology.
2. **Great Fracture:** freedom and imposed order collide, causing collapse.
3. **Forgetting:** survivors conceal knowledge in living traditions, places and stories.
4. **Awakening:** contemporary people uncover legacies and unexpected abilities.
5. **New Lemuria:** future generations choose whether to heal or repeat the division.

## Seven principles
Earth — regeneration and interdependence; Water — memory and adaptation; Fire — transformation and courage; Air — perception and communication; Light — discovery and awareness; Sound — resonance and creativity; Unity — connection without coercion. **Every power has a limitation, an ethical dilemma and a cost.**

## Proposed feature-film trilogy
**Film I — The First Light:** discovery, gathering and the first warning. **Film II — The Great Fracture:** the moral catastrophe and scattering of knowledge. **Film III — The New Dawn:** converging histories and the choice between freedom and enforced unity. This is a proposed packaging structure; the twelve productions are not necessarily twelve episodes of one season.

## Animated extensions
Young Guardians (ages 4–7): kindness and wonder. Sevenfold Quest (8–12): teamwork and ecosystems. Fracture Chronicles (13–17): persuasion, truth and governance. Convergence Stories (adult/family): layered intergenerational mystery.

## Seven immersive realms
Living Forest; Waters of Memory; Architects’ Citadel; Valley of Fire; Temple of Resonance; Worldbuilders’ Village; Heart of Lemuria.

## Studio and rights approach
Majestic Studios is a proposed production home, with Black Mountain Ranch considered as a possible nature-integrated campus. Acquisition, permits, funding, cast, distribution and construction are not secured. Keep a canonical story bible, rights ledger and approvals process. Consider founder-controlled IP ownership separate from property and project financing, subject to qualified legal advice.
