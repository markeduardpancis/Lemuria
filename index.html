The mythology

A world remembered.
A future imagined.

Across five ages — First Harmony, Great Fracture, Forgetting, Awakening and New Lemuria — an ancient civilization's legacy returns through people who never expected to carry it. Seven principles shape the world: Earth, Water, Fire, Air, Light, Sound and Unity.

An interconnected story engine for feature films, character-led series, animation and immersive experiences.
